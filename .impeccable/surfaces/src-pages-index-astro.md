## Direction contract
THESIS: One hairline-ruled sheet that reads like a precise technical record: numbered, dated, cited. It refuses the consultant template of portrait hero, service cards, logo strip and testimonials.
OWN-WORLD: Cool white paper, graphite ink, hairline rules, one carrier-green square LED reserved for now and selected states, a hatched square for aged content. Martian Mono, narrowed, for headings, numbers, dates and citation keys; Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for prose. A left gutter axis with functional section numbers, two-column record rows with mono outcome lines, bracketed citation keys resolving to References, square outlined controls, authored SVG arrows.
STORY: The visitor learns Sandip builds AI systems that hold up in production, believes it from the dated and sourced record, picks a door (founder, AI adopter, speaking or press) and sends an enquiry.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Masthead rule with name and London at the content edge, five mono nav links at right. Gutter axis with 01 on the headline baseline. Mono headline "I build AI systems that hold up in production." up to 4.5rem over three lines, a one-sentence lede, the green-LED Now status strip, then a filled graphite "Start an enquiry" button beside two text links, all within 40rem. The right third stays open paper.
FORM: User-pinned reference killenberger.com rendered light, overriding re-roll assignment candidate 4 (RFC protocol spec), whose structure (shared numbering, citations, now and aged marks) binds the open dimensions. Seed key ac31b5b0.
SIGNATURE: The gutter lights the current section with a green square on the axis and copies section links on click. The enquiry topic choice lights its LED and rewrites the prompt and next-step line. Motion: a slow LED breath on the Now status and 250 to 350ms exponential ease-out colour transitions; nothing hidden by default.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
